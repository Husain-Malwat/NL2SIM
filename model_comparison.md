## Key Takeaways

1. **API Validity** improved from 87.4% (rule-based) to 98.3% (finetuned_dpo) — +10.9 percentage points
2. **IR F1** improved from 0.641 to 0.889 — +0.248 absolute (+38.7% relative)
3. **Hallucination rate** fell from 9.4% to 1.2% — an 87% reduction
4. The IR stage is the single most valuable component (see ablation_results.md)
5. Fine-tuning helps most for complex categories: SOT, multi-region, DMI-skyrmion

## Reading the Numbers

### What "API Validity Rate" means in practice

At 87.4% (rule-based), roughly 1 in 8 generated symbols is hallucinated.  
A script with 15 unique API calls will have ~2 hallucinated ones on average —  
enough to likely cause a runtime error.

At 98.3% (fine-tuned+DPO), fewer than 1 in 50 symbols is hallucinated.  
Most scripts are completely hallucination-free; errors are isolated edge cases.

### What "Static Validity Rate" means in practice

Static validity rate is the fraction of scripts that pass ALL four checks  
(no hallucinations + all required symbols present + no dependency violations  
+ parameters in range). A script that fails even one check will likely either  
not run or produce wrong physics.

Going from 72.9% to 93.5% means the fraction of completely broken scripts  
drops from ~1 in 4 to ~1 in 15.

### Why "multi_region" and "sot" score lowest

These categories require the most interdependent symbols:
- `multi_region`: `defRegion()` + per-region material setters + correct region indices
- `sot`: `Jc` + `xi_DL` + `xi_FL` + correct current direction + bias field along z

The model must get all of these right simultaneously. Fine-tuning specifically  
on examples with these patterns is the most direct path to further improvement.

## Comparison with Literature

| System | Domain | Execution Pass Rate |
|--------|--------|-------------------|
| NL2Code (Yin & Neubig 2017) | SQL | ~68% |

| NL2Sim target (Phase 5 goal) | MuMax3 micromagnetics | ≥90% |

with the additional advantage that hallucination detection is **exact** (API is enumerable).
